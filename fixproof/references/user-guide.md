# Fixproof user guide

Tell your NAS what broke. It tells you whether you are covered, what the manual says to try, and what to send the manufacturer.

## Who it is for

Anyone who keeps receipts, warranty cards and manuals on a UGREEN NAS, or scans or photographs them there, and never remembers which one matters when an appliance fails. Families, renters, landlords and home-office users.

## What you can say

- "My dishwasher is leaking and showing E15."
- "Is my washing machine still under warranty?"
- "The fridge stopped cooling. Am I covered?"
- "When does the TV warranty run out?"
- "Which of my warranties expire in the next three months?"

## What you get

1. **Coverage verdict.** Covered, partly covered, not covered or unclear, with the exact end date and one line of reasoning. Protection plans bought separately are included. They are the ones people forget they paid for.
2. **Fix it now.** The troubleshooting steps for your symptom or error code, taken from your own manual, with the safety warning first and technician-only steps marked.
3. **Claim draft.** Model, serial, purchase proof, fault description and the claim contact, filled in from your documents. Saved to your NAS only if you say yes. The skill never sends it.
4. **Evidence list.** The exact file every fact came from, so you can open and check it.
5. **Warranty calendar** (sweep mode). Every appliance with a receipt, sorted by when its coverage ends.

## How it works

Receipt (files and photos) gives the model and serial. Warranty and plan documents are then searched by that model, not by a guess. Dates are computed from the real purchase date and the real term. The manual is searched by the model series, and only the rows that match your symptom are shown. The receipt, warranty card and rating-plate photo are cross-checked, and any mismatch is reported.

## Safety and privacy

- Read-only. It never deletes, moves or overwrites files. The only writes are the claim draft or calendar note, after you confirm.
- Nothing leaves your NAS. It never sends, uploads or shares documents.
- If a receipt, warranty or manual is missing, it says which one and does not make anything up.
- For gas, smoke, burning smells or water near electrics, it tells you to switch off and call a technician before anything else.

## Tips

Any folder works, but folders named Receipts, Warranty, Manuals and Scans are searched first. A clear phone photo of the appliance's rating plate lets the skill confirm the serial number on your warranty registration.
